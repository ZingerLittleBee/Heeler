Heeler ships these icons from @lobehub/icons-static-svg 1.95.0 (lobe-icons
commit ce4861a27514b4704f748498befe682d4597fd55) as the marks of the Agent
programs it lists:

antigravity, antigravity-color, amp, amp-color, claudecode, claudecode-color,
cline, codex, codex-color, cursor, devin, devin-color, geminicli,
geminicli-color, githubcopilot, grok, hermesagent, kilocode, kimi, kiro,
kiro-color, mastra, opencode, qoder, qoder-color, qwen, qwen-color

Modifications: sizes and fills are fixed for the asset catalog and path data
is rewritten with explicit separators. Codex's color mark drops its white
backing; Gemini CLI's keeps only its prompt glyph; Kiro's is its ghost in Kiro
purple; Kiro's, Qoder's and Qwen's color marks have dark-appearance variants;
and Antigravity's color mark is rasterized.
